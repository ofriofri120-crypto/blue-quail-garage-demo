Garage door spring repair

Springs do the heavy lifting for your garage door. When one breaks you usually hear a loud bang from the garage, and the door will not open or opens only a few inches.

Signs your spring is broken

  • A visible gap in the spring above the door
  • The opener strains or stops after a few inches
  • The door feels very heavy when you lift it by hand
  • The door closes too fast

What we do

We replace torsion and extension springs, check the cables, drums and rollers, and balance the door so the opener does not wear out early. Most doors with two springs should have both replaced at the same time.

How long does it take?

Most spring replacements take about an hour.
